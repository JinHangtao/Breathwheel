Breathwheel · Meditative Listening Space

Breathwheel is a meditative listening space by Jin Hangtao: an interactive generative mandala that breathes with you. Hold to breathe, speak or play your own music, and the light and ripples respond in real time. Best experienced with headphones. Your voice stays on your device and is never uploaded or recorded to the cloud. 一个用于冥想与试听的空间：由呼吸、声音与音乐驱动的生成式曼陀罗。声音仅在本地处理，不上传、不记录至云端。
